Junkers Ju 88

Feldflugplatz Holm · late summer 1944

Twin Jumo 211s, four tanks, four SC 250s. Walk to the aeroplane, climb in, take off from the strip, bomb the supply depot to the east, and bring it back onto the concrete. Each engine is started on its own. Watch oil temperature — a Jumo sitting at full throttle on the ground will seize.

  • W A S D walk · Enter board the Ju 88
  • 1 2 start / stop engines
  • Q E or ← → throttle
  • W stick forward, nose down · S stick back, nose up · W brake on ground
  • Stall long enough and it spins. Nose down to recover. A hard landing burns.
  • A D turn
  • F flaps · Space bombs

Aerodynamics

Stick. W pushes the nose down. S pulls it up. This is a stick, not “W to climb”.

Throttle. Q and E move a lever that stays where you leave it. More power climbs and goes faster in level flight; it does not set a speed by itself.

Flaps. Takeoff flaps lower the speed you need to rotate. Landing flaps add drag and cap speed — a descent with the boards out bleeds IAS instead of building it. Clean (UP), a nose-down run picks up speed and can pass level-flight cruise.

Energy. Climbing costs knots. Pushing over with flaps up turns height into IAS. Level out and the extra speed bleeds off. Idle with the nose down and flaps up, you accelerate; same dive with landing flaps, you slow down.

Stall and spin. Clean stall is about 125 km/h. Flaps buy a slower stall. Hold the nose up as speed falls: it mushes, then it spins. Recover by pushing the nose down (W) until it flies. Spinning in, or arriving too hard, starts a fire.

Engines. Each Jumo starts on its own. They drink from the tanks on their side first. Full throttle with the wheels on the concrete will cook the oil and seize. Airspeed cools them. One engine out yaws the aeroplane.

Takeoff. Flaps TO, throttle up, S to rotate around 140 km/h.

Landing. Flaps LDG, bleed speed, put it on the grey strip. Flare with S so the rate of descent dies before the concrete. W is the wheel brake once you are down. Stop on the strip to complete.

Bombs. Four SC 250s. Space releases one. Empty racks, or three buildings wrecked, and you are on the way home.
